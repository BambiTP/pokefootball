PokéFootball

Types: any
How to use & notes

Drag onto a position — or tap a Pokémon, then tap a position. Drag a placed one back here to remove it. Turning on All gens (or using any Pokémon past Gen 1) turns the field blue; a Pokémon used twice gets a red circle. Toggles that are on show up on the saved image.

Note: Flying types can't fly higher than 5 feet.

Note: Most Pokémon can jump really high.
